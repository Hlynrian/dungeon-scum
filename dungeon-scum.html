Traits, equipment & bonuses

Enter applicable trait, gear and situational bonuses before rolling. Melee: include +1 for a Stunned target. At most one situational-advantage die. Minions ignore attack bonuses; missile caps: 2 / 3 / 3 / 5. Stunned subtracts one action die. Armour defence is automatic; other defence dice are prompted.

Table reminders & special rolls

Stun clears after the following activation. A second stun downs a Minion; others ignore it. A wounded Hero is also Stunned; a second wound downs them. Track wound and stun separately.

Movement buttons show a raw dice total, not distance; hover over the total in chat to inspect individual dice. Apply the movement rules in their chat notes. A failed missile attack has no effect; a failed melee attack makes the defender retreat. Roll defence separately and resolve net hits manually.
